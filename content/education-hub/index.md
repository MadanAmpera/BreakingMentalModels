levels:
  - lab: Level 01 · The lens of the self
    rn: I
    title: Individual
    desc: How people perceive, feel, decide and behave at work — one nervous system at a time.
    ct: 14 theories
    cat: i
    theories:
      - num: i.01
        title: Self-determination theory
        sub: Deci & Ryan, 1985
        summ: Why autonomy, competence and relatedness are not motivators — they are the conditions in which motivation appears.
        tags: [Motivation, Foundational]
      - num: i.02
        title: Expectancy theory
        sub: Vroom, 1964
        summ: Effort is a multiplication, not an addition — and any term going to zero collapses the whole.
        tags: [Motivation]
      - num: i.03
        title: Equity theory
        sub: Adams, 1963
        summ: People do not compare themselves to ideals. They compare themselves to the person two desks over.
        tags: [Motivation, Fairness]
      - num: i.04
        title: Goal-setting theory
        sub: Locke & Latham, 1990
        summ: Specific, difficult goals beat "do your best" — almost always, and especially when the work is dull.
        tags: [Performance]
      - num: i.05
        title: Job characteristics model
        sub: Hackman & Oldham, 1976
        summ: A job's design — skill variety, task identity, autonomy, feedback — does more for motivation than any motivational speech.
        tags: [Design]
      - num: i.06
        title: Big Five personality
        sub: Costa & McCrae, 1992
        summ: Five trait dimensions that explain a surprisingly large amount, and also a surprisingly small amount, of who shows up at work.
        tags: [Personality]
      - num: i.07
        title: Attribution theory
        sub: Heider · Kelley · Weiner
        summ: When their behaviour is the issue it's their character; when yours is, it was the situation. The "fundamental" mistake.
        tags: [Perception]
      - num: i.08
        title: Cognitive dissonance
        sub: Festinger, 1957
        summ: We do not act on what we believe. We come to believe what we have just done.
        tags: [Cognition]
      - num: i.09
        title: Affective events theory
        sub: Weiss & Cropanzano, 1996
        summ: Small events at work produce small emotions; small emotions accumulate into the year you're having.
        tags: [Emotion]
      - num: i.10
        title: Self-efficacy
        sub: Bandura, 1977
        summ: The single best predictor of whether someone will try the hard thing is whether they believe they can.
        tags: [Belief]
      - num: i.11 — i.14
        title: Four further theories
        sub: Reinforcement · Decision-making heuristics · Job crafting · Emotional labour
        summ: Each with its own reading and, where available, video lesson.
        tags: [Browse all →]
  - lab: Level 02 · The lens of the room
    rn: II
    title: Group
    desc: How collections of people coordinate, conflict, lead and follow — the workplace as a room of rooms.
    ct: 14 theories
    cat: g
    theories:
      - num: ii.01
        title: Psychological safety
        sub: Edmondson, 1999
        summ: The single condition without which most other team interventions are theatre.
        tags: [Foundational, Voice]
      - num: ii.02
        title: Tuckman's stages
        sub: Tuckman, 1965
        summ: "Forming, storming, norming, performing — and the underrated fifth: adjourning, often skipped at great cost."
        tags: [Team development]
      - num: ii.03
        title: Social identity theory
        sub: Tajfel & Turner, 1979
        summ: The group is not just where you work; under the right conditions, it is who you become at work.
        tags: [Identity]
      - num: ii.04
        title: Groupthink
        sub: Janis, 1972
        summ: Cohesion is good for output and bad for judgement. The team that agrees too quickly should be the team you worry about.
        tags: [Decision-making]
      - num: ii.05
        title: Transformational leadership
        sub: Bass, 1985
        summ: The four "I"s — idealised influence, inspirational motivation, intellectual stimulation, individualised consideration.
        tags: [Leadership]
      - num: ii.06
        title: Servant leadership
        sub: Greenleaf, 1970
        summ: Lead by spending your authority on the people you lead, not on the room.
        tags: [Leadership]
      - num: ii.07
        title: LMX theory
        sub: Graen, 1976
        summ: There is no "the team". There are as many teams as there are dyads. Lead the relationships, not the org chart.
        tags: [Leadership]
      - num: ii.08
        title: Conflict modes
        sub: Thomas & Kilmann, 1974
        summ: Compete, accommodate, avoid, compromise, collaborate. None is always right; all are usually misused.
        tags: [Conflict]
      - num: ii.09
        title: Communication networks
        sub: Bavelas, 1948
        summ: Who can talk to whom, in what shape — wheel, chain, all-channel — quietly decides what your team can become.
        tags: [Communication]
      - num: ii.10
        title: French & Raven's bases of power
        sub: "1959"
        summ: Five sources — coercive, reward, legitimate, referent, expert. Most workplaces over-use the first three.
        tags: [Power]
      - num: ii.11 — ii.14
        title: Four further theories
        sub: Social loafing · Diversity & faultlines · Distributed leadership · Team reflexivity
        summ: Each with its own reading and, where available, video lesson.
        tags: [Browse all →]
  - lab: Level 03 · The lens of the system
    rn: III
    title: Organisational
    desc: How structure, culture, and the long environmental conditions of work shape everything that happens inside them.
    ct: 14 theories
    cat: o
    theories:
      - num: iii.01
        title: Schein's culture model
        sub: Schein, 1985
        summ: Artifacts, espoused values, basic assumptions — and the depressing distance between each layer.
        tags: [Culture, Foundational]
      - num: iii.02
        title: Mintzberg's configurations
        sub: Mintzberg, 1979
        summ: Five basic shapes the organisation can take. Most dysfunction is a shape mismatch.
        tags: [Structure]
      - num: iii.03
        title: Burns & Stalker
        sub: "1961"
        summ: Mechanistic vs organic structures — and the conditions under which each one breaks the other.
        tags: [Structure]
      - num: iii.04
        title: Resource dependence theory
        sub: Pfeffer & Salancik, 1978
        summ: Organisations are shaped, daily, by the things they need but do not control.
        tags: [Environment]
      - num: iii.05
        title: Institutional theory
        sub: DiMaggio & Powell, 1983
        summ: Why organisations in the same field come to look uncannily alike — and what that costs them.
        tags: [Environment]
      - num: iii.06
        title: Organisational learning
        sub: Argyris & Schön, 1978
        summ: Single-loop vs double-loop learning — and why most "lessons learned" sessions only do the first.
        tags: [Learning]
      - num: iii.07
        title: Lewin's change model
        sub: Lewin, 1947
        summ: Unfreeze, change, refreeze. Still the cleanest map of why change initiatives don't stick.
        tags: [Change]
      - num: iii.08
        title: Kotter's 8-step model
        sub: Kotter, 1996
        summ: The famous staircase — best used not as a recipe, but as a checklist of things you forgot.
        tags: [Change]
      - num: iii.09
        title: Competing values framework
        sub: Quinn & Rohrbaugh, 1983
        summ: Clan, adhocracy, market, hierarchy. Most workplaces try to be all four and end up being none.
        tags: [Culture]
      - num: iii.10
        title: Sociotechnical systems
        sub: Trist & Bamforth, 1951
        summ: Technology and social structure must be designed together, or one will silently eat the other.
        tags: [Systems]
      - num: iii.11 — iii.14
        title: Four further theories
        sub: Sensemaking · Ambidexterity · Organisational identity · Practice theory
        summ: Each with its own reading and, where available, video lesson.
        tags: [Browse all →]
